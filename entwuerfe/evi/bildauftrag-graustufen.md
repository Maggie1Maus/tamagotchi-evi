## Der Auftrag

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone character design for a black-and-white virtual pet game — grayscale shading exploration, preview only, no code changes.

The attached image is the EXACT CURRENT EVI REFERENCE. It is a strict 1-bit black-and-white pixel art drawing. Your task is NOT to redesign her — it is to redraw her in the same style but with grayscale shading added. Every single feature of her design must be preserved exactly.

IDENTITY TO PRESERVE (do not change any of these):
- Wide foxlike ears, slightly tilted outward, with solid black inner areas and lighter outer edges
- Small forehead tuft between the ears
- Large round face, slightly wider than tall
- Large glossy black eyes with a square white highlight in the upper-left of each eye
- Cute open smile showing a small white tongue
- Fluffy pure white neck ruff / chest fur, wide and soft
- Dotted / stippled fur texture across the body and face
- Bushy upward-curving tail on the viewer's right, with a white tip
- Seated pose, body compact, tiny front paws visible at the bottom, facing the viewer directly

WHAT I WANT — GRAYSCALE VERSION:
Redraw this exact character using the full grayscale range from pure black to pure white, with smooth intermediate gray tones. The character's silhouette, proportions, and all identifying features stay identical. Only the rendering changes: instead of strict 1-bit (black or white only), use shading.

Specific shading guidance:
- Main body fur: soft mid-gray base, slightly lighter on the belly and chest, slightly darker on the back and flanks
- Neck ruff / chest fur: nearly white with very subtle light-gray shadows underneath and at the edges to show volume and fluffiness
- Inner ears: dark gray (not solid black like the reference — softer, with a slight gradient toward the tip)
- Ear outer edges and forehead tuft: soft dark-gray pixel-style outlines, same shape as reference
- Eyes: remain mostly black with the characteristic square white highlights — this must stay exactly as in the reference
- Smile / tongue: same as reference, just integrated into the shaded face
- Body outline: dark gray, consistent weight, pixel-art style (stepped hard edges, not smooth ink lines)
- Fur dots: rendered as soft gray stippling instead of hard black dots — same pattern, softer tone
- Tail: mid-gray body with a slightly darker outer curve, white tip with a soft gray underside shadow
- Shadow under the body: very faint light-gray cast shadow on the ground, optional

STYLE CONSTRAINTS:
- Still clearly pixel art / retro handheld game aesthetic — think Game Boy Advance or early DS sprite style
- Stepped pixel outlines (no smooth anti-aliased ink lines)
- No color whatsoever — strictly black, white, and grays
- No background, no room, no props, no ground texture other than a minimal shadow
- White background
- No text, no watermark, no UI, no frame, no signature
- The character must feel like the same Evi — a viewer familiar with the reference must immediately recognize her

FORMAT:
Square or slightly portrait orientation. Character large and centered, taking up roughly 70-80% of the image height. High enough resolution to be clearly readable when scaled down to around 60x80 pixels for a small game screen.
```
